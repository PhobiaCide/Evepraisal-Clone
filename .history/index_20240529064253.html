What is this?

Evepraisal is a tool for quickly getting bulk estimates of items from Eve Online.

How does this work?

Find something in Eve Online that you want to price check like a contract, cargo scan result, etc. Press CTRL + A to Select all, CTRL + C to copy, then click on the lovely box to the left or above this and press CTRL + V to paste. It's pretty standard. I'm sure you're familiar with it. Submit and you should see a price breakdown of all the items.

What all can I paste in there?

Inventory Cargo Scanner D-Scan Bill of Materials Loot History Planetary Interaction Survey Scanner View Contents Contracts EFT Blocks In-game Killmail Wallet Transactions Asset listings (named ships don't work) Manual Entry - Example: "94812 Tritanium"
